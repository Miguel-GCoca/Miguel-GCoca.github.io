**Mechanical Engineering, University of Central Florida, Class of 2026**
</div>

I'm Miguel Grisales Coca, a recent grad from the University of Central Florida. My passion is robotics, I've been taking apart electronics and repairing devices my whole life. My first job was working the register at Chick-fil-A at 15 years old, just to save up enough to buy my first 3D printer. My interests and skills cover the full robotics stack. That includes CAD design, fabrication, embedded systems, circuit design, ROS2 and sim to real. I like to take a project all the way from concept to reality, meaning I'll acquire whatever knowledge and skills I need to bring my projects to life.

My favorite project I've been part of was an AI controlled robot dinosaur. It was trained with reinforcement learning to walk in simulation before the body was built. Read more about it on my [projects page](/).

My research experience spans three labs. In the Assistive Robotics Lab I was in charge of mechatronics engineering, CAD design, rapid prototyping and fabrication. In the EMBRACE Lab I pioneered a study that used wearable technology to research fall risk in older adults. The WEAR Lab put me in charge of multiple projects developing wearable sensing and human augmentation devices. That work led to co-authoring a publication in *Frontiers in Aging* and two conference posters.

I'm now looking for my first full time role as a mechatronics, mechanical or simulation engineer, ideally one that keeps me close to automation, 3D printing, rapid prototyping, and robotics in industry.
