**WEAR Lab, University of Central Florida — July 2023**

A cable-driven system to measure human gait response to trips and slips by suddenly and safely pulling on the ankle.

**Role — CAD Designer**
- Created an 80/20 frame in SolidWorks and used FEA to prove a factor of safety of two with minimal deflection.
- Routed the cable system to pull an ankle in any planar direction.
