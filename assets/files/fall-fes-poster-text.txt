aims and methods:
Aim 1: Design and fabrication of a balance perturbation system to induce
slip/trip-like perturbations during treadmill walking
➢ Method: Implement a bump-emulation system (i.e., bump’e,m) onto a
treadmill, in conjunction to a truss with safety harness, to simulate gait
perturbation during walking and record data via force sensor and IMU.
Aim 2: Developing a pre-impact fall detection algorithm to detect loss of
balance after unexpected slip/trip-like perturbations using wearable sensors
(IMU & pressure insoles)
➢ Method: Selection of fall indicator parameters, identifying number of
sensors and optimal sensor placement position, selection of classifier
method and validation
Aim 3: Assisting/augmenting balance recovery response to restore
gait/postural stability after pre-impact fall detection using a lower limb hybrid
FES-exoskeleton system
➢ Method: Design and fabrication of the wearable robotic device and
developing a hybrid control algorithm combining feedback control for
robotic actuator and feedforward control for FES.

Background and motivation:
• In the U.S, more than 1 out of 3 older
adults aged ≥ 65 years fall each year [1].
• Successful balance recovery at the onset
of a fall requires quick & strong lower limb
reflex responses, involving high rate of
torque development (RTD) [2].
• Age-related changes in muscle contractile
properties result in a decline in RTD during
fast voluntary contractions.
• This research aims to assist lower limb
balance recovery response in older adults
at the onset of a fall, by simultaneously
providing assistive torque at joint level and
Functional Electrical Stimulation (FES) at
muscular level.
Pre-impact Fall Detection and Balance Recovery Assistance using Lower Limb 
Hybrid FES-Exoskeleton
Renoa Choudhury, Nicolette Fournelis, Miguel Grisales Coca, Mark Aldritz Dela Virgen, Joon-Hyuk Park
Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, UCF

Objectives:
1. Develop a lower limb hybrid FESexoskeleton to assist balance recovery at
fall onset- integrating FES with robotic
actuators
2. Evaluate the efficacy of the proposed
system in assisting the user to recover
balance against unexpected slip/trip-like
perturbations in controlled environment

significance and borader impact:
• Successful implementation will result in
reducing fall related injuries & deaths,
enhancing healthy longevity, and improving
quality of life among elderly
• Once validated, it may reduce financial burden
of health care sector by minimizing the
medical and rehabilitation expenditure
associated with fall
